## Editorial rules

- Keep one clear primary intent per page.
- Write for importers, roasters, distributors, and coffee businesses rather than for search engines.
- Use the primary phrase naturally in the title, H1, introduction, and relevant internal links.
- Do not repeat a phrase unnaturally or create near-duplicate pages for singular and plural keyword variations.
- Link informational articles to the relevant origin, product, export, and inquiry pages.
- Update claims, dates, specifications, and availability only when the underlying business information changes.
